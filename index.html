Our story

Once upon a time…

Once upon a time, a small round world floated through the internet, smiling at everyone who passed by. One day, a lost traveler stopped to ask for directions, and the little world did something unusual: it helped, and asked for nothing back.

Word spread, more travelers arrived, and each one left a bit of kindness behind, a tip here, a laugh there, a hand up when someone needed it. Bit by bit that kindness built E2, a place where everyone's welcome and everyone has something to give.

Pull up a seat, friend. The world keeps smiling because of people like you. 🌍

Make yourself at home

Welcome to the world of E2! 🌍 We're really glad you're here. Take a minute to say hi in #introductions, tell us what you're into, and don't be shy about asking questions.

  1. Read the E2 code. It's short and kind, promise.

  3. Pick your roles. Choose your interests to see the right channels.

Not sure what to say? Try one of these

  • What's one small thing that made you smile today?
  • What's a skill you'd love to learn, and one you could teach someone else?
  • Share a win this week, big or tiny. We'll cheer for you.
  • What's the best piece of advice anyone's ever given you?
  • Recommend something you love right now: a song, a show, a game, or a tool.
  • What's something you're working on that we could help with?

The E2 code

Short, kind, and easy to live by

  1. Be kind. Treat everyone the way you'd want to be treated.
  2. Help where you can. Questions are welcome and nobody gets laughed at for asking.
  3. Keep it safe for everyone. No hate, harassment, or NSFW content.
  4. No spam or unsolicited self-promo. Share your stuff in the right channel.
  5. Respect privacy. Don't share anyone's personal info, including your own.
  6. Listen to the mods. They're here to keep E2 a good place to be.

If something feels off, ping a mod. We'd rather hear it than not. 💛

FAQ

Questions? Good, we love those.

What is E2?

E2 is a Discord community, the world of E2. It's a friendly, generous place where people help each other, share what they know, and try to make each other's days a little better.

Who can join?

Anyone who's happy to follow the E2 code. Whether you're into gaming, creative stuff, tech, side hustles, fitness or music, there's room for you. You'll need a Discord account.

I'm shy. Do I have to talk?

Not at all. Lots of people start by quietly reading along. Say hi when you're ready. Someone here will have your back.

I don't think I have anything to teach. Can I still join?

Of course! "Still figuring it out" is a perfectly good answer. Good vibes and encouragement count too, and everyone has something to give.

How do I take part in the Kindness Streak?

From Nov 13 to Dec 1, 2026, do the day's kind act, then log it in the server with a 💛. Missed a day? Just pick up again the next day.

When are events?

The times on this page are examples in Philippine Time (UTC+8). Final times will be announced in the server.

How can I help E2 grow?

Bring one kind human with you! Share the invite with a friend who'd love a community like this, and help welcome new people when they arrive.
